## 6. HERO RECOMMENDATION — cinematic drone approach

### Composition target

`raja_1.jpeg` remains the **final resting composition** and is treated as a spec, not a mood:
hero ≈ 92svh; copy column left at ~45% width; structure occupying the right; `EST. 1977` chip
above an 3-line display headline with "Luxury" italic in accent blue; two CTAs; nav floating
transparent over the hero; overall high-key/light; generous negative space at the left.

`raja_2.jpeg` is a **second, previously unreviewed board** showing an *interior* looking-up
composition. It is a legitimate alternative and should be shown to the owner alongside
raja_1 — it reads warmer and more "event", where raja_1 reads colder and more "engineering".

### The camera choreography you described — concrete spec

Five beats, one continuous eased path, **~7–9 seconds** on first load, then handing over to
scroll. All values are proposals for approval, not implemented.

| Beat | Camera | Reveals | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Establish | high + far, ~70 m out, 35° above horizon, looking down the long axis | site context, the structure as an object on ground | 0.0 – 2.0 s |
| 2 · Approach | dolly in along the axis, descending to ~25 m, pitch easing to 15° | bay rhythm, roof geometry, span | 2.0 – 4.5 s |
| 3 · Descend | drop to ~11 m, eave line crosses the frame top | membrane, purlins, eave detail, human scale | 4.5 – 6.0 s |
| 4 · Enter | pass the gable plane; interior volume opens | interior volume, seating, staging | 6.0 – 7.5 s |
| 5 · Settle | ease to the **raja_1 resting frame** and hold; hand off to scroll-linked drift | final composition | 7.5 – 9.0 s |

**Motion rules:** single continuous cubic-bezier path, no cuts; velocity peaks in beat 2 and
decays monotonically (so it *arrives* rather than stops); no roll, no shake, no FOV punch;
maximum yaw across the whole move ≤ 25°. Skippable on any user scroll input — the first scroll
jumps straight to the resting frame.

**Mandatory fallbacks:** `prefers-reduced-motion` → render the resting frame immediately, no
fly-through. No WebGL → static poster image of the resting frame. Mobile → begin at beat 4
(short descend + settle) rather than the full path, at reduced DPR with shadows off.

**Sequencing caution:** a 9-second intro delays the headline. Recommendation — the **headline
and CTAs render immediately at full opacity** and do not wait for the camera; only the
structure animates behind them. A government procurement visitor must never wait to read who
this is.
